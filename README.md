# 📊 HR Analytics & Workforce Retention Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze employee demographics, departmental headcount, attrition drivers, and internal career progression. Designed to help HR teams identify turnover trends, evaluate promotion fairness, and make data-driven talent decisions.

## 🎯 Key Business Questions Answered
* **Workforce Distribution:** How is headcount distributed across departments, job roles, and locations?
* **Attrition & Retention:** What is the overall attrition rate, and which departments or tenure groups experience the highest turnover?
* **Promotion & Career Growth:** Which departments and job levels have the highest promotion rates, and how long do employees wait between promotions?
* **Retention Impact of Growth:** How strongly does time since last promotion correlate with employee attrition and tenure?
* **Demographics & Diversity:** What is the breakdown of employees and promotions across age groups, gender, and education levels?

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technical Skills Demonstrated
* **Power BI Desktop:** Multi-page interactive report design, custom visual formatting, drill-through pages, and dynamic slicers.
* **Power Query (ETL):** Data cleaning, handling missing values, data type standardization, and column transformations.
* **Data Modeling:** Star-schema relationships and custom date/calendar tables for time-intelligence analysis.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Custom calculated measures for Attrition Rate %, Promotion Rate %, Average Years Since Last Promotion, Active Employees, and KPI variance indicators.
