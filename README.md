# 📊 HR Analytics & Workforce Retention Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze employee demographics, departmental headcount, attrition drivers, and workforce retention metrics. Designed to help HR teams identify turnover trends and make data-driven talent decisions.

## 🎯 Key Business Questions Answered
* **Workforce Distribution:** How is headcount distributed across departments, job roles, and locations?
* **Attrition & Retention:** What is the overall attrition rate, and which departments or tenure groups experience the highest turnover?
* **Demographics & Diversity:** What is the breakdown of employees by age group, gender, and education level?
* **Performance & Satisfaction:** How do job satisfaction, work-life balance, and salary bands correlate with employee retention?

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technical Skills Demonstrated
* **Power BI Desktop:** Interactive report design, custom visual formatting, drill-through pages, and dynamic slicers.
* **Power Query (ETL):** Data cleaning, handling missing values, data type standardization, and column transformations.
* **Data Modeling:** Star-schema relationships and custom date/calendar tables for time-intelligence analysis.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Custom calculated measures for Attrition Rate %, Active Employees, Average Tenure, and KPI variance indicators.
